OUR STORY

Keeping Indigenous Seeds Alive

African Indigenous Seeds is a community-rooted agritech initiative founded by Lilian Chepngetich Kenduywa in May 2025 in Seme, Kisumu County, Kenya.

I started this initiative to help conserve indigenous seed varieties, strengthen farmers' access to affordable planting materials and preserve traditional seed knowledge for future generations.

Through community engagement, seed collection, selection, cleaning and storage, I am working to keep valuable local seed varieties available to farming communities.

OUR COMMUNITY IMPACT

Small Seeds. Meaningful Impact.

300 Farmers reached

These figures reflect the community work and progress achieved through the initiative.

WHAT I DO

Conservation Through Community Action

Seed Conservation

Collecting, selecting, cleaning and preserving indigenous vegetable, cereal, fruit and tree seeds.

Farmer Access

Supporting access to locally conserved planting materials through community engagement and initial seed sales.

Youth Engagement

Involving young people in seed cleaning, handling and storage while building awareness of agricultural heritage.

OUR NEXT STEP

Building an Indigenous Seed Marketplace

I am developing a digital platform designed to connect seed savers, farmers and buyers, making indigenous planting materials easier to discover and access.
